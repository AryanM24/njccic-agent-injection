New Jersey Civilian Cyber Resilience Corps

Font Size: + -
Feedback Print

Strengthening New Jersey's Cybersecurity Posture Through Volunteer Excellence

The New Jersey Civilian Cyber Resilience Corps is a volunteer force of skilled cybersecurity professionals dedicated to enhancing our state's cyber defense capabilities. Operating under the New Jersey Cybersecurity and Communications Integration Cell (NJCCIC), the Corps provides essential support to public sector entities and critical infrastructure providers, helping them prevent, respond to, and recover from cyber incidents.

By leveraging the expertise of volunteer professionals across the state, we create a powerful resource that strengthens New Jersey's resilience against evolving cyber threats.

Join the Corps: Volunteer Your Expertise

Are you a seasoned cyber professional with 3+ years of experience? Help protect New Jersey's critical services and infrastructure. Your skills in incident response, threat modeling, and defense are invaluable.

Mission and Services

NJ CIVILIAN CYBER CORPS MISSION

The Cyber Corps serves as a volunteer-based cybersecurity force that augments New Jersey's cyber defense and incident response capabilities. We assist public and private entities in improving their cyber resilience and recovering from adverse cyber events.

SERVICES WE PROVIDE

  • Cybersecurity Assessments: Comprehensive evaluations of security posture and vulnerabilities
  • Target Hardening: Implementing measures to strengthen defenses and improve resilience
  • Incident Response & Recovery: Rapid deployment to contain, eradicate, and recover from cyber incidents
  • Cybersecurity Training: Educational programs to enhance awareness and capabilities
  • Strategy & Policy Development: Guidance on creating effective cybersecurity frameworks
  • Threat Intelligence Sharing: Coordinating information to prevent future attacks

Note: All services are provided at no cost to eligible New Jersey entities, ensuring that critical infrastructure and public agencies can access expert cybersecurity support regardless of budget constraints.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • Professional Experience: Minimum 3 years in cybersecurity, IT systems administration, network engineering, incident response, or related fields
  • Technical Competency: Demonstrable knowledge of operating systems, networking, and cybersecurity tools
  • Certifications (Preferred): Industry certifications such as Security+, CISSP, OSCP, or equivalent
  • Background Check: Consent to criminal background screening by NJOHSP
  • Employer Support: Letter of support from current employer (if applicable)

WHAT WE OFFER VOLUNTEERS

  • Professional development through advanced training and exercises
  • Training and sponsorship opportunities for relevant certifications
  • Access to the NJCCIC cyber range for hands-on practice
  • Networking with cybersecurity leaders across public and private sectors
  • Liability protection under state and federal volunteer protection laws
  • Health and Accident Insurance coverage during deployments
  • New members receive a comprehensive orientation covering Corps operations, protocols, and tools.
  • Annual requirements include participation in at least two NJCCIC training programs or exercises to maintain readiness.
  • All deployments are voluntary - you choose when and how to contribute based on your availability.

HOW TO APPLY TO BE A CYBER CORPS VOLUNTEER

Send an email to the NJCCIC with your resume and a cover letter stating your reasons for applying to be a Cyber Corps Member to njccic@cyber.nj.gov.

Request Assistance
WHO CAN REQUEST CYBER CORPS SERVICES?

The Cyber Corps provides assistance to:

  • State & Local Government: All governmental entities, including state departments, agencies, counties, municipalities, and school districts
  • Critical Infrastructure: Healthcare systems, utilities, transportation networks, and other essential services
  • Educational Institutions: State and county colleges, public schools, and charter schools
  • Public Authorities: Utility authorities, commissions, and other public entities
  • Non-Profit Organizations and Small Businesses: Pending available resources
WHEN TO REQUEST SUPPORT
  • During active cybersecurity incidents requiring immediate response
  • For proactive security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • When developing or updating cybersecurity policies and procedures
  • To conduct cybersecurity awareness training for staff
  • Following an incident for recovery and resilience improvement
HOW DEPLOYMENT WORKS

The NJCCIC evaluates requests based on severity, scope, impact, and available resources. Once approved, volunteer Corps members are mobilized to work directly with your IT staff. Deployments are typically short-term, focusing on stabilizing immediate threats or completing specific support tasks.

HOW TO REQUEST ASSISTANCE

Governance and Operations

ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

The Cyber Corps operates under the New Jersey Cybersecurity and Communications Integration Cell (NJCCIC) within the Office of Homeland Security and Preparedness (NJOHSP). This structure ensures integration with state cybersecurity infrastructure and coordination with key agencies.

ADVISORY COUNCIL

An Advisory Council of cybersecurity leaders from public and private sectors provides expert guidance on:

  • Program policies and best practices
  • Training curriculum development
  • Volunteer eligibility standards
  • Strategic partnerships and collaboration

OPERATIONAL PROTOCOLS

  • 24/7 Availability: Emergency requests handled through a dedicated hotline
  • Rapid Deployment: Alert system for quick volunteer mobilization
  • Secure Communications: Encrypted channels and secure portals for sensitive information
  • After-Action Review: Comprehensive debriefs to capture lessons learned

NJCCIC SECURITY OPERATIONS CENTERS

The NJCCIC’s primary Security Operations Centers (SOCs) are located in West Trenton and Newark. The SOCs also maintain capabilities for remote operations and on-site deployments as needed.

Legal Protections and Agreements

VOLUNTEER PROTECTIONS

Corps members are protected under:

  • Federal Volunteer Protection Act: Shields volunteers from personal liability for good faith actions
  • State Law Protections: Additional New Jersey statutes protecting volunteer service
  • Liability Waivers: Service recipients agree to hold harmless all Corps volunteers
  • State Insurance: Health and Accident Insurance coverage during deployments

INFORMATION SECURITY

Multiple layers of protection ensure confidentiality to the extent permitted by law, including:

  • Incident Reporting Law: Information protected under P.L.2023, c.19
  • Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act (CISA) of 2015
  • Relevant OPRA exemptions

BEFORE DEPLOYMENT, SERVICE RECIPIENTS SIGN AGREEMENTS COVERING:

  • Confidentiality of shared information
  • Access provisions and safety protocols
  • Liability waivers and indemnification
  • Data breach notification responsibilities
  • Intellectual property protections

Note: These protections do not extend to willful misconduct, gross negligence, or criminal acts. All volunteers must adhere to professional standards and ethics.
