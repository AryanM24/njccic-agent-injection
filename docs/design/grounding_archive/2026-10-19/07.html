our vision

Elizabeth Public Schools will be one of the highest performing urban school districts in the nation.

our promise

To provide an innovative and personalized learning environment that ensures that every child achieves excellence.

our goals

College preparedness, career readiness for every child by implementing Multi-Tiered Systems of Supports, Professional.

Full Strategy Map

Teaching & Learning

Enhance teaching and learning to provide ALL students with personalized pathways to success.

Social & Emotional Wellness

Support the social emotional wellness of ALL students and staff to promote a safe environment in which ALL feel a sense of belonging.

Workforce Development

Hire, develop, and retain staff to establish a highly effective and diverse workforce.

Parent & Community Engagement

Strengthen the engagement of ALL members of the Elizabeth Public Schools Learning Community to improve outcomes for students and their families.

Organizational Effectiveness

Promote a culture of excellent customer service and continuous improvement across the district.
