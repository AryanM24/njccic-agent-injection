Technology Help Desk

West Windsor-Plainsboro Regional School District » Departments » Technology » Technology Help Desk
To create a help desk ticket:

Students (grades 4-8): Please report the technology issue to a staff member so that a ticket may be submitted on your behalf.

Students (grades 9-12): Please use the ticket kiosk in the media center to submit your technology issue. Remember to include your wwprsd.org email in the ticket description

Staff: Please ensure that you are connected to the secure network. If you are not in District, be sure to utilize Global Protect to create a secure connection to the District network.

Staff link to the Technology Help Desk Ticket System (once connected to the secure network): http://k1000.westwindsor.k12.nj.us/
