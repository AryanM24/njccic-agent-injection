Each coder undertakes:

  form, including by copying them off the project machine, photographing them,

- Not to use the material against any real system, or in any training corpus.

  until both sheets are finished, and never to open `answer_key.csv`.
- To delete any personal copies when coding ends and confirm deletion to the
  PI.
- To report to the PI at once any loss, exposure or misuse of the material.
